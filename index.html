Hello, I’m Tania! On this website, you can see a selection of my work as a graphic designer and digital artist.

I’m based in Vinnytsia, Ukraine, and in 2026 I graduated with honors with a bachelor’s degree in software engineering from Vinnytsia National Technical University. I work part-time at the university, creating infographics, posters and presentations in Figma, Photoshop and Canva, and I previously designed posters for its Central Student Election Committee. In my free time, I make personal digital art and Live2D animations. I have also won prizes in graphics and IT competitions, including second place at the American-Ukrainian computer graphics competition in 2025.

Academic project

Software for 4D Figure Projections

Presentation Design · Bachelor’s thesis

A defense presentation for my bachelor’s thesis on developing software that creates projections of 4D figures. It covers the research goals, a comparison with existing tools, the software architecture, the mathematics of rotations and projections, and the interface. Complex technical material is turned into clear diagrams, tables and infographics.

Research project

Media Phenomenon of Spartak Subbota

Presentation Design · 2023

A research presentation about the media phenomenon of the blogger Spartak Subbota and the public scandal around his credentials. It combines text analysis, interview quotes, media screenshots, a comparison table of news coverage and survey charts, and closes with conclusions on media literacy.

A tri-fold brochure for LOFT, an imaginary café. Made in Adobe Illustrator as one of the tasks for my Photoshop and Illustrator certificate. Deep green and gold give it a warm, cozy feel, and it is designed to be printed.
